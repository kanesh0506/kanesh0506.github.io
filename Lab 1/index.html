Key Strategic Insights

  • Rapid Expansion: Population expanded over 3x between 1970 and 2026, driven by post-independence healthcare improvements and economic urbanization.
  • Rising Non-Citizen Share: Non-citizens grew from ~280k in 1970 to over 3.37M by 2026, contributing to key labor sectors.
  • Demographic Transition: Median age is climbing steadily while child cohorts (0-14) shrink relative to working-age groups.
