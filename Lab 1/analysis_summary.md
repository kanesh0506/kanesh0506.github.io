# Malaysia Demographic & Population Analysis Report (1970–2026)

## 1. Key Executive Findings
- **Total Population Growth**: Malaysia's population grew from 10.88M in 1970 to 34.39M in 2026 (+216% total growth over 56 years).
- **Ethnic Composition (2026)**:
  - **Bumi Malay**: 18.13M (52.7%)
  - **Chinese**: 6.83M (19.9%)
  - **Bumi Other**: 3.82M (11.1%)
  - **Non-Citizen Residents**: 3.37M (9.8%)
  - **Indian**: 2.01M (5.8%)
  - **Other Citizen**: 0.24M (0.7%)

## 2. Age Profile Dynamics
- **Peak Age Bracket**: 25–29 years old (3.13M) followed by 20–24 years old (3.12M).
- **Working-Age Population (15–64)**: Comprises ~70% of the total population, representing a strong economic demographic dividend.
- **Aging Population Trend**: Population aged 65+ accounts for over 2.88M individuals, reflecting gradual population aging.
